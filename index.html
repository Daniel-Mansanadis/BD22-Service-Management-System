From Initial Consultation to Complete Fire Protection.

A straightforward, organized, and transparent engineering workflow from initial site assessment to final turnover.

Phase 01: Consultation & Inquiry

Submit your project details, site location, and initial fire protection scope through our direct inquiry form.

Phase 02: Site Assessment & Survey

Our technical team reviews building specifications and conducts an on-site hazard and water-supply evaluation.

Phase 03: Proposal & Engineering Plan

We deliver an itemized engineering proposal detailing recommended suppression systems, scope of work, and schedule.

Phase 04: Procurement & Installation

Upon approval, our certified personnel manage materials, pipe fabrication, and systematic site installation.

Phase 05: Hydrostatic Testing & Turnover

Rigorous hydrostatic testing, alarm verification, and sign-offs are completed alongside BFP-ready documentation.

Phase 06: Preventive Maintenance

Active warranty protection, preventative maintenance agreements, and periodic testing ensure continuous system readiness.
