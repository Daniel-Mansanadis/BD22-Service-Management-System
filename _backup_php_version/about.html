About BD22 Fire System & Builders Inc.

Engineering life safety, code compliance, and reliable fire suppression across high-value residential, commercial, and industrial properties in the Philippines.

SAFETY • INTEGRITY • EXCELLENCE

Our Commitment to Life Safety & Structural Resilience

BD22 Fire System & Builders Inc. was founded on the fundamental principle that modern building safety demands uncompromising precision. Fire incidents are swift and destructive; our engineering solutions are designed to react instantly and reliably.

Specializing in fire sprinkler hydraulic calculations, clean agent gas suppression (FM-200 and Novec 1230), deluge systems, and intelligent addressable alarm networks, we work side-by-side with general contractors, facility managers, and safety directors across Metro Manila.

1

Technical Rigor & Code Compliance

Every system we design and install is strictly compliant with the Revised Fire Code of the Philippines (RA 9514) and NFPA 13, 20, 25, 72, and 2001 international standards.

2

Certified Engineering Personnel

Our on-site supervisors, licensed mechanical and electrical engineers, and certified pipefitters undergo continuous factory certification and safety training.

3

Preventative & Emergency Reliability

We don't just build systems and walk away. Through our scheduled PMS contracts, we guarantee rapid emergency response, quarterly valve audits, and full warranty support.
