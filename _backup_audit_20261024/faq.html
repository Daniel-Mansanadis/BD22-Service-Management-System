1. Fire Safety Standards & Installation

All BD22 systems are fully engineered to comply with the Revised Fire Code of the Philippines (RA 9514), National Building Code, and National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) standards including NFPA 13 (Sprinkler Systems), NFPA 14 (Standpipes), NFPA 20 (Stationary Fire Pumps), NFPA 72 (Fire Alarm & Signaling), and NFPA 2001 (Clean Agent Fire Extinguishing Systems).

Yes. We assist clients from initial Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance (FSEC) through final Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC) turnover. Our certified engineers furnish signed and sealed hydraulic calculations, as-built drawings, and pump performance test certificates required by local BFP inspectors.

Depending on square footage, most commercial warehouse retrofits range from 3 to 8 weeks. We specialize in phased, off-peak, and weekend installations to ensure that your facility remains fully functional with zero disruption to daily logistics operations.

2. Customer Portal & Service Management

When you submit a service request, an automated Reference Code (e.g. INQ-2026-1048) is generated. You can log into your BD22 Customer Portal anytime to track the live review status, assigned technician, and scheduled site visits in real time.

Inside the Customer Portal, navigate to the "Warranty" tab. There you will see all active warranties for installed alarm panels, valve assemblies, and fire extinguishers along with exact expiration dates and warranty terms.

Active service contract holders have access to our 24/7 hotline and rapid portal dispatch. Urgent tickets immediately alert our standby on-call engineering team for expedited on-site diagnostic response.
